# Privacy Policy **Last Updated: 14/01/2026** ## 1. Introduction peaq ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). **By using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.** ## 2. Information We Collect ### 2.1 Health Data (via Apple HealthKit) The App accesses health and fitness data from Apple HealthKit with your explicit permission. This data includes: - **Activity Data**: Steps, active energy burned, exercise time, distance - **Heart Rate Data**: Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV) - **Sleep Data**: Sleep analysis and duration - **Fitness Data**: Workouts, VO2 Max - **Vital Signs**: Blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂), body temperature - **Body Measurements**: Body mass (weight) **Important**: We only access health data that you explicitly authorize through Apple's HealthKit permission system. You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device's Settings. ### 2.2 Device Information We may collect information about your device, including: - Device model and operating system version - App version - Device identifiers (used anonymously for analytics) ### 2.3 Usage Data We may collect information about how you use the App, such as: - Features accessed - Time spent in the App - Crash reports and error logs ### 2.4 Account Information If you create an account or provide personal information, we may collect: - Email address (if provided) - User preferences and settings - Profile information (age, gender - if provided) ## 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: - **Provide the Service**: Analyze your health data to generate performance scores, insights, and recommendations - **Improve the App**: Understand usage patterns to enhance features and fix bugs - **Personalize Experience**: Customize recommendations and insights based on your data - **Ensure App Functionality**: Process and display your health metrics and analytics - **Comply with Legal Obligations**: Meet legal requirements and respond to lawful requests ## 4. Health Data Processing and Storage ### 4.1 Local Processing **Most of your health data is processed locally on your device.** Our algorithms analyze your HealthKit data directly on your iPhone or iPad. This means your sensitive health information may never leave your device in raw form. ### 4.2 Data Storage - **HealthKit Data**: Your raw health data remains in Apple HealthKit under your control. We do not store copies of your raw HealthKit data on our servers. - **Processed Metrics**: We may store aggregated, anonymized metrics (such as performance scores) to improve our algorithms and provide features like historical trends. - **Cloud Storage**: If you enable cloud sync or backup features, processed metrics may be stored in encrypted cloud storage associated with your account. ### 4.3 Data Retention - We retain processed metrics and analytics for as long as you use the App and for a reasonable period thereafter (typically 90 days after account deletion) unless you request earlier deletion. - You can delete your account and associated data at any time through the App's settings or by contacting us. ## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure ### 5.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data **We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal health data to third parties.** ### 5.2 Limited Sharing We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances: - **Service Providers**: With trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the App (e.g., cloud storage, analytics providers) under strict confidentiality agreements - **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, court order, or government regulation - **Business Transfers**: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (users will be notified) - **With Your Consent**: When you explicitly authorize us to share information ### 5.3 Anonymized Data We may use anonymized, aggregated data (that cannot identify you) for: - Research and development - Improving our algorithms - Statistical analysis - Public health research (with appropriate safeguards) ## 6. HealthKit Data Rights ### 6.1 Your Control - **You control all HealthKit permissions** through your device's Settings app - You can grant or revoke access to specific health data types at any time - When you revoke HealthKit permissions, the App will stop accessing that data immediately ### 6.2 HealthKit Data Storage - Your health data is stored securely in Apple HealthKit, which is encrypted and protected by Apple's security measures - We do not have direct access to your HealthKit data storage - Apple's HealthKit privacy and security policies apply to the underlying data storage ## 7. Data Security We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information: - **Encryption**: Data in transit and at rest is encrypted using industry-standard protocols - **Access Controls**: Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis - **Secure Authentication**: Secure authentication methods for account access - **Regular Security Reviews**: Ongoing security assessments and updates However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. ## 8.
